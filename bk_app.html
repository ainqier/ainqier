How to use this in four steps
  1. Add a statement. Choose a PDF, CSV or Excel file (or several), or paste the rows. Text PDFs work. Scanned pictures do not.
  2. Check the result. Look at the sign check, the balance check and the rows kept. Use the column choices if something was picked wrongly, and tap "This did not read right" if it still looks off.
  3. Review the flagged items. Give uncategorised payees a category once and it is remembered. Remove rows you do not want, or restore them from the removed list.
  4. Export. Dashboard is one finished sheet. Full workbook adds every transaction, T-accounts, the journal, a one-page client summary, recurring payments and an audit trail. Clean transactions is just the cleaned rows. Save session lets you pick the work up later.

Everything is read on your device. Nothing is uploaded, except that the PDF reader loads from the internet the first time.

Privacy

Your file stays here

The file is read and cleaned inside your browser tab. Nothing from your statement is uploaded, and closing the tab clears it. Two small exceptions: PDFs are read by a reader library fetched from a public code host (your file is not sent to it), and the category rules you choose to teach are saved in this browser so they are remembered next time. You can delete them from the Categories list.

Ainqier cleans formatting, suggests categories and flags likely duplicates. It does not know your chart of accounts and does not replace reviewing the result against the source statement. Sample data is fictional. Not tax or accounting advice.

Amounts in the workbook use the J$ format of the dashboard template.
