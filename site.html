The cleaning

What it fixes

The usual problems in statement exports, each one shown before and after.

Mixed date formats

One column, five formats. Numeric dates are read as month-first or day-first from the whole column, not row by row.

4-Mar-2024MAR 5 20242024-03-04

Amounts in any notation

Brackets, trailing minus, CR and DR suffixes, currency symbols and European decimal commas become one signed number.

(45.20)12.99 DR1.234,56-45.20

Noisy descriptions

Transaction prefixes, terminal codes and store numbers come off. The original text stays in its own column.

CHECKCARD 0306 SHELL OIL 57444Shell Oil

Blank and summary rows

Empty lines, page breaks and total rows are dropped so they cannot reach your import.

Total this pageremoved

Duplicate lines

Exact repeats are listed for review. Two real identical purchases on one day are possible, so you decide.

88.0088.00flagged

A balance check

Enter the opening and closing balances and Ainqier says whether the cleaned rows prove to the statement.

Privacy

Your file stays here

The file is read and cleaned inside your browser tab. Nothing from your statement is uploaded, and closing the tab clears it. Two small exceptions: PDFs are read by a reader library fetched from a public code host (your file is not sent to it), and the category rules you choose to teach are saved in this browser so they are remembered next time. You can delete them from the Categories list.
